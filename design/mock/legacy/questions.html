Questions

Straight answers.

60 questions owners ask before they book, answered plainly. If yours is not here, ask it on the call.

Book your free audit

The basics.

What does the audit cost?

Nothing. It is free, and you keep the plan whether or not you work with us.

What happens in the audit?

You bring rough numbers: leads a month, your average job, how many quotes you close. We walk your business from the first call to the review, including after hours, and show you where jobs are going.

Do I have to buy a package?

No. There are no packages. We build what your audit calls for and nothing else.

How is it priced?

Every fix is scoped after the audit, only if you want it, and priced to your business’s size.

Do you run ads?

No. Everything we build works on leads and customers you already have.

Who builds it?

Sam does. He builds and watches every client’s system himself, and takes on a few businesses at a time.

Where do I see what it is doing?

In one app on your phone. Every reply, booking and follow-up is there.

Does it work with the tools I already use?

Yes. It connects to your calendar, your books and your lead sources.

Front desk.

Phone agent: Will callers know it isn’t a person?

Yes. It introduces itself as your business’s assistant. We don’t pretend it’s a person.

Phone agent: Can it send calls to me?

Yes. You choose which calls ring through, such as emergencies or existing customers.

Website chat: What does it know?

Only what you give it: your services, area, hours and pricing rules.

Website chat: What if it gets a question wrong?

It hands off instead of guessing, and we tune it from the transcripts.

Messages and DMs: Which channels?

Facebook, Instagram and Google Business messages. Others on request.

Messages and DMs: Does it post for me?

No. This only answers messages. Posting is a separate build.

Lead vetting: Who decides what counts as a fit?

You do. We turn your rules into the questions.

Lead vetting: What happens to the ones that are not a fit?

They get a clear, polite answer, so nobody is left waiting.

After hours: Who is on call?

Whoever you set, on a rota you control.

After hours: What counts as an emergency?

Your rules. We write them with you.

Calendar.

Reminders: Text or email?

Text first. Email as well if you want it.

Reminders: Can I change the wording?

Yes. It should sound like you.

Scheduling and dispatch: Does it replace my scheduling software?

No. It works with what you use and does the arranging for you.

Scheduling and dispatch: Can I override it?

Always. It proposes, you decide.

Job updates: Where does the timing come from?

Your calendar and, if you want, the crew marking a job started and finished.

Job updates: Does the crew need a new app?

No. One tap in the app you already have, or a text.

Quotes.

Estimates: How accurate is the draft?

It is a starting point built from your prices. You check and adjust every one.

Estimates: Which trades?

Any trade with a price book. We set it up around how you measure and price.

Proposals: Can it use my branding?

Yes. Your logo, your colours, your terms.

Proposals: Does it chase the proposal?

That is the Follow-up build. They work together.

Money.

Invoices: Do you handle the money?

No. Payments go through your own provider. We send and chase the invoice.

Invoices: Can I stop reminders for one customer?

Yes, in one tap.

Bookkeeping prep: Does this replace my bookkeeper?

No. It does the sorting so they can do the accounting.

Bookkeeping prep: Which accounting software?

We work with what you and your bookkeeper already use.

After the job.

Review replies: Does it post without me?

You choose. Many owners let good reviews post and approve anything under four stars.

Review replies: Can it remove a bad review?

No. Nobody honest can promise that.

Win-backs: Is this spam?

No. It goes only to people who have dealt with you, and every message has a way to stop.

Win-backs: How often?

Rarely. A couple of well-timed messages beats a monthly blast.

Service reminders: I do not do repeat work. Is this for me?

Maybe not. The audit will say.

Service reminders: Can customers opt out?

Yes, with one reply.

Referrals: Do I have to offer a reward?

No. Plenty of people refer without one.

Referrals: When does it ask?

Only after a positive review or reply.

Email and text campaigns: Is this advertising?

No. We do not run ads. This goes only to your own customers.

Email and text campaigns: How do people stop getting them?

One reply or one click.

Getting found.

Local search: Will this get me to number one?

Nobody honest can promise a ranking. This does the work that tends to help.

Local search: Is this ads?

No. We do not run ads.

Job photos to posts: Do customers have to agree?

We set it up so nothing identifying goes out without permission.

Job photos to posts: What if a photo is bad?

It is skipped, or it waits for your approval.

Team and office.

Hiring: Does it decide who I hire?

No. It asks your questions and books the call. You choose.

Hiring: Where do applicants come from?

Wherever you post. This handles what happens after they apply.

Team assistant: Where does it get the answers?

Only from what you give it. It does not make up company policy.

Team assistant: Who can use it?

Whoever you add. You can limit what each person sees.

Field notes: Does the crew need training?

No. If they can leave a voicemail, they can do this.

Field notes: What about poor signal?

It sends when the phone is back in range.

Reports: Can I pick what is in it?

Yes. We start from the standard set and change it to suit you.

Reports: Where does the data come from?

The tools you already use, connected during setup.

Call review: Is recording calls allowed?

Rules vary by state. We set it up to follow yours, including telling callers.

Call review: Who sees the calls?

Only the people you choose.

Custom.

Custom agents: What can an agent do?

Read, write, look things up, fill in forms and use the tools you connect. It works inside limits you set.

Custom agents: What will it not do?

Spend money or send anything you have marked for approval without asking.

Custom builds: How do I know if it can be built?

Ask. If it cannot be done well, we will say so.

Custom builds: How long does it take?

It depends on the job. You get a timeline with the scope.
