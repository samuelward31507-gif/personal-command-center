# Cinder client app audit, November 16 2026

Two inputs: a read of the whole client app (`app/(client)/app`, `lib/client-*`, the API routes and public pages), and
research on what contractors complain about in Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Workiz, GoHighLevel, ServiceM8,
FieldEdge, JobNimbus, AccuLynx and Leap. The design side is in `docs/design-spec.md`.

## Fixed today (verified in the code before fixing)

| Bug | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Export everything shipped an empty customers.csv: `details->>address` without quotes made the query fail and the error was swallowed. The one file a nervous switcher opens first. | `app/api/app/export/route.ts` uses `details->>'address'`. |
| A cell that owns one business and is crew in another got owner rights in both. Owner sessions were also never re-checked, so a changed owner cell kept working for 90 days. | `clientFor` (`lib/client-app-run.ts`) works out the role per business on every request; the export route re-checks after it. |
| A web form, Angi, Thumbtack or Facebook lead that arrived before the business number was live was answered 202 and never saved. | `app/api/intake/route.ts` saves it as the owner's lead and alerts them; nobody is texted. |

## Where Cinder already beats them

- Speed to lead on every channel, with the agent replying and booking. Jobber's and Housecall Pro's AI answers but does
  not chase; GoHighLevel chases but can't run the job. Cinder does both in one product.
- Lead to paid on one board. Jobber shipped a sales pipeline only in April 2026 and it stops at quotes.
- Import with dedupe, preview and 7-day undo, and nobody is texted during an import. Competitors' guides tell people to
  re-key recurring jobs by hand.
- Export everything as one zip (now that it works).
- No payment lock-in: customers pay through the owner's own link.
- Leads by email with the Gmail confirmation handled, which covers Yelp, Houzz and Google LSA today.

## Connections: one place that says what is connected

Today Settings, Connections mixes lead sources, outbound tools and a log. Status is real (from lead events and send
failures) for every row except two.

| Source | Today | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Speed-to-lead agent | Settings, Agent: wording and a preview | No on/off switch for the whole business and no status row. On/off exists only per conversation. First thing an owner looks for. |
| Missed-call text-back | Twilio; row says Live whenever a number exists | Status is assumed. Forwarding is never verified, A2P state never downgrades it, the test texts the owner rather than calling the number. |
| Website form | Webhook with key, test, real status | Email-only submissions are rejected (no phone). Should go to the "needs a phone" queue like lead emails. |
| Website chat | Snippet, real status | No test. |
| Facebook and Instagram lead ads | Zapier or Make webhook | No native connect. Zapier polls every 5 to 15 minutes, which defeats speed to lead. |
| Google Ads lead forms | Missing | Google posts `user_column_data` to a webhook URL with a key. Small to add. |
| Google Local Services | Lead emails only | No sub-source, no call leads. LSA's API needs Google partner access. |
| Angi | Native webhook | Fine. |
| Thumbtack | Zapier | Thumbtack has a partner API; email covers it meanwhile. |
| Yelp, Houzz | Lead emails | Fine for now. |
| Lead emails | New, real status | Needs the Resend inbound setup and deploy. |

## Switching over: what still breaks

- Customers without a phone are skipped on import, with their open invoices. Commercial and email-only customers are
  common in Jobber and QuickBooks.
- Payment history comes in as one lump "paid before the move" row per invoice. No payments CSV.
- Plans import only from a Jobber pull, frequency guessed as monthly. No plans CSV for Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan.
- Jobber attachments come in as links in notes. Jobber's file links expire, so the photos are effectively lost.
- Team members are not imported.
- Nothing tells the owner their old quote and invoice links stop working, and there is no "resend from Cinder" step.
- Phone number: forwarding only, no port request flow.
- The review link can only be set by staff, not the owner.
- Contractor-facing copy still says "Ask Sam" or "with Sam" in about 33 places, and one error mentions the agency's
  setup sheet. Fine for clients we onboard ourselves; wrong for a self-serve switch.

## Day to day: gaps against a top CRM

- Email is send-only. Customer replies land in the owner's own inbox, not on the timeline.
- No dispatch view: no crew-by-day board, drag to reschedule, map or route.
- The owner can't fix a forgotten clock-out (the server action exists, no screen calls it).
- No offline mode. A crew in a basement gets a blank app. Jobber has the same gap and contractors complain about it.
- No error boundary in the app.
- In demo mode the Export links on Switch and Invoices go to the real API and show a sign-in error.
- Crew can read and reply to every lead's texts, not just their jobs. May be intended; worth a setting.

## Ranked plan

Effort: S under a day, M one to three days, L a week or more.

### Now (stops a switch from failing)
1. Agent master switch and an "Agent: On, last reply 12m ago" row at the top of Connections. S
2. Import customers without a phone, with their invoices. M
3. Website-form leads without a phone go to the "needs a phone" queue instead of being rejected. S
4. Owner can set their review link in the app. S
5. Replace "Ask Sam" copy in contractor-facing screens with self-serve steps. S
6. Guard demo-mode export links; owner screen for editing time entries; app error boundary. S

### Next (connections that sell the product)
7. Connections redesign per the spec: one table, real status, a drawer per source with steps, test and log. M
8. Real missed-call status: Needs setup until a forwarded missed call is seen, a "call your number now" test, A2P state on the row. M
9. Google Ads lead-form webhook. S to M
10. Native Facebook and Instagram lead ads (Meta app, page subscription, leadgen webhook). M to L, needs a Meta app review.

### Then (switch-over depth)
11. Download Jobber attachments into Cinder during the pull, before links expire. M
12. Payments CSV and plans CSV (Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan). M
13. Team import. S to M
14. Go-live step: "your old links stop working", resend open quotes and invoices from Cinder, porting vs forwarding with a port request form. M

### Later (beat them on what they're worst at)
15. Two-way email on the timeline (inbound replies through the same Resend receiving). M
16. Dispatch board: crew by day, drag to reschedule, route order. L
17. Offline crew app: cached shell, checklists, photos and time clock queued and synced. L
18. Pricing page promise: flat shop price, free crew seats, month to month, self-serve cancel, one-click export.
    Competitors' biggest complaints are price cliffs, hidden add-ons and lock-in. Product and copy, not code.
19. Done-for-you A2P registration with a status on the Connections row, and a toll-free fallback while it's pending.

## Table stakes a switching contractor checks on day one

Customer records with history, notes and photos; online booking and request forms; quotes with e-sign and follow-ups;
invoices with online pay, deposits and reminders; a schedule; recurring jobs and service plans; a crew app with time,
checklists, photos and on-my-way texts; two-way texting and email; review requests; a customer portal; QuickBooks
Online sync; Angi, Thumbtack and LSA intake; revenue and job reports; consumer financing (by link).

Cinder has all of these except a drag-and-drop schedule, two-way email and a native LSA connection.
