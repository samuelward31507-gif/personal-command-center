How it works

Fifteen minutes, then it’s yours. No slides and no pitch deck. One call about how your business runs, and a short report you keep whether or not you work with us.

1

Book a time.

Pick a slot. Bring rough numbers: leads a month, your average job, how many quotes you close.

2

We walk your business.

Calls, forms, texts, estimates, quotes, invoices, reviews and the work done by hand, including after hours.

3

You keep the plan.

What it’s costing you, a number on each finding, and what to fix first.

What you leave with

A short report with a number on everything. Made from your business, not from a template.

  1. Every finding, mapped.Each place a job or an hour can slip, checked against how you run today.
  2. A figure on each.Worked out from your own numbers: leads a month, average job, how many quotes you close.
  3. The order to fix them.Ranked by what each one costs you, so the first fix is the one that pays most.
  4. A plan that’s yours.Take it to anyone, or build it yourself.
