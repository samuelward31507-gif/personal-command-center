The problem1 of 5

An unanswered review still says something.

People read the replies as closely as the stars. A good review with no reply looks ignored, and a bad one with no reply looks true.

How you know you have it

Sign 1

Reviews from months ago with no reply

Sign 2

A bad review you heard about from a customer

Sign 3

Replying on one site and not the others

People read the replies as closely as the reviews.

How it works2 of 5

Two reviews, handled two ways.

Here is what it does, in order. The drawing here shows the same thing happening.

Step 1Replies that sound like you

Not the same sentence under every review.

Step 2Bad reviews come to you

With a suggested reply and the job history.

Step 3Every platform

Google first, others if they matter in your trade.

Step 4Nothing sits unanswered

Including the ones from last year.

What changes3 of 5

Every review is answered, and the hard ones come to you first.

Nothing about how you run jobs has to change. This is the difference on an ordinary day.

Without it

  • Reviews sit with no reply.
  • A bad review catches you off guard.
  • "Thanks for the review" forty times.

With it

  • Each one is answered.
  • You see it first, with a draft.
  • A real reply each time.
