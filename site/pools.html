How this is worked out

We assume a share of inquiries hires someone faster: 2% if you reply within 5 minutes, 10% within the hour, 20% the same day and 30% the next day or later. Those lost inquiries times your win rate times your job size is the first number. If quotes aren't always chased twice, we assume consistent follow-up would win 10% more of the jobs you quote. These are conservative estimates, not promises. The audit replaces them with your real numbers.
