Front desk / Website chat

Visitors get an answer while they're still on the page.

A chat on your site answers questions about your services and your area, then offers a time for an estimate.

Website chat
The problemHow it worksWhat changesGetting set upQuestions

01 The problem

Your site gets questions when nobody is there to answer them.

People look at contractors in the evening. If the only way in is a form or a phone number, the easy move is the back button.

How you know you have it

  1. 1

    Visits in the evening, forms the next day or never

  2. 2

    The same three questions by phone every week

  3. 3

    A contact form as the only way in

02 How it works

A question at 10 PM, answered and booked.

Here is what it does, in order. The drawing at the top of the page shows the same thing happening.

  1. FirstAnswers from your own information

    Services, service area, hours and how you price.

  2. ThenOffers a time

    When someone is ready, it books the estimate instead of asking them to call.

  3. NextKnows when to stop

    Anything it can't answer goes to you with the conversation attached.

  4. LastWorks after hours

    Most people look at night. The chat is there when they do.

Someone is on your site at 10 PM with one question. They get an answer.

03 What changes

The visitor leaves with a time, not a number to call tomorrow.

Nothing about how you run jobs has to change. This is the difference on an ordinary day.

1

They leave to find a phone number.

They ask on the page.

2

The form sits until morning.

The estimate is booked tonight.

3

You answer the same five questions.

It answers them for you.

04 Getting set up

A short list from you. The rest is on us.

We build it around the tools and habits you already have, and you see it working before it touches a real customer.

What we need from you

  • Your hours and service area
  • The questions you ask a new lead
  • Who gets the call when it needs a person

What we take care of

  • Set it up around the number and pages you already use
  • Test it with you before it takes a real lead
  • Watch the first weeks and adjust

It starts with the free audit. If the audit does not call for this build, we will say so.
