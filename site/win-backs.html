01 The problem

Your past customers have lost your number.

They liked the work. They would hire you again or pass your name on. But years go by, nobody writes, and the next job goes to whoever shows up in a search.

How you know you have it

  1. 1

    A customer list that only lives in old invoices

  2. 2

    Repeat work that comes in by accident

  3. 3

    Slow months with nobody to call

02 How it works

A year of past customers, each written to at the right time.

Here is what it does, in order. The drawing here shows the same thing happening.

  1. FirstYour old list, cleaned up

    Customers and quotes from your books, texts and spreadsheets.

  2. ThenA reason to write

    A season, an anniversary of the job, or a quote that never got an answer.

  3. NextReplies come back to you

    As leads, booked the same way as new ones.

  4. LastIt stops when asked

    One reply and they are off the list.

The cheapest lead you will ever get is someone who already hired you.

03 What changes

People who already trust you hear from you again.

Nothing about how you run jobs has to change. This is the difference on an ordinary day.

1

Old customers forget your name.

They hear from you once or twice a year.

2

Dead quotes stay dead.

Some of them come back.

3

More leads means more ad spend.

More leads from a list you already own.

04 Getting set up

A short list from you. The rest is on us.

We build it around the tools and habits you already have, and you see it working before it touches a real customer.

What we need from you

  • Your customer list, however messy
  • What you want to be known for
  • A yes before anything is sent

What we take care of

  • Clean the list up
  • Write the messages in your voice
  • Honor every opt-out

It starts with the free audit. If the audit does not call for this build, we will say so.

05 Questions

The questions we get on every audit call.

The short answers.

Is this spam?

No. It goes only to people who have dealt with you, and every message has a way to stop.

How often?

Rarely. A couple of well-timed messages beats a monthly blast.

Is this part of a package?

No. There are no packages. We build what your audit calls for and nothing else.
