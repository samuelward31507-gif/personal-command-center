The problem

Nights and weekends are when the urgent calls come. An emergency and a quote request sound the same to voicemail. One needs someone tonight. The other needs a time next week. Without a way to tell them apart, you get woken for everything or miss the one that mattered.

How you know you have it

  1. Being on call for every call
  2. Emergencies you found out about in the morning
  3. Weekend quote requests answered on Monday
