The problem

Finished work you haven’t been paid for is a loan you never agreed to. Invoices wait until you have time to send them, and chasing them is awkward enough that it gets put off. The money was earned weeks ago.

Friendly first, firmer later. And never from you personally.

Day 3

Hi, just a reminder that the invoice for your gate install is ready. You can pay from this message.

Day 10

Your invoice for the gate install is now a week past due. Here is the link to settle it.

Day 21

This invoice is three weeks past due. Please pay today or reply so we can sort it out.

They stop the moment it is paid. You can stop them for one customer in one tap.
