The outage rush.

  • Every missed call and web form gets a text back in about 10 seconds, day or night
  • It asks your questions, like home size, fuel type and timeline
  • It books the site visit on your calendar, inside the hours and limits you set
  • Anything it isn’t sure of, or any job over your limit, comes straight to you

The quotes that go quiet.

  • Every install quote followed up on days 2, 5 and 10, then monthly until they answer
  • The homeowner picks the unit and add-ons, signs and approves on their phone
  • The deposit is paid on your own payment page, straight to you
  • “Too expensive” gets your financing option or a smaller unit, and real price talk comes to you

After the install.

  • Each generator logged with its model, serial number and warranty dates
  • Annual service as a plan: the reminder text, the visit and the invoice on a schedule
  • A review request the day after the job

What doesn’t change.

What happens when 40 people call in one day?

Every one gets a text back in seconds, whether you pick up or not. It qualifies each home and offers site-visit times from your calendar, so you spend the day on the jobs that are ready, not on voicemail.

Do I have to change software?

No. Cinder sits in front of what you use now. It catches and books the lead, and you run the job where you always have.

Which plan fits a dealer?

If the outage rush is the leak, Never Miss a Lead. If install quotes going quiet is the bigger one, Book & Close adds the quote follow-up, the signed approval and the deposit step. See pricing and guarantees.
