Our story

The phone was never the job.

Why service businesses lose work they already earned, and what we do about it.

[Chapter 01]

The story

A service business has always been two jobs. Most shops only have someone for the first one.

Job oneThe work

The work itself. The thing you are good at, and the reason customers call.

Job twoThe desk

Answering, booking, quoting, chasing, invoicing, asking for the review. None of it is the trade. All of it decides whether you get paid for the trade.

Then the desk got bigger. Leads used to come from one phone. Now they come from calls, texts, forms, Facebook, Google and lead sites, at any hour, and each one expects an answer in minutes.

78%

of people have taken their business elsewhere after a company didn’t pick up.

CallRail, 2025
21x

MIT / InsideSales lead response study
69%

Quo, 16M+ calls, 2026

And it all lands on someone who is on a job. Leads don’t wait for a good time. They come while you are on a job, at dinner, and after you have gone to bed.

Five things the desk needs that a busy owner can’t give it.

  1. SpeedA reply while they still care, not tonight.
  2. CoverageNights, weekends, and every hour you are on a job.
  3. MemoryThe quote from three weeks ago. The invoice from last month.
  4. HandoffsCall to calendar to quote to invoice to review. Every step is a place to drop it.
  5. TimeYours. There is only so much of it.

None of that is skilled work. All of it costs jobs.

[Chapter 02]

The approach

The desk can run without a person now. AI can read a message, answer it, book the time, draft the estimate, chase the quote and ask for the review. It does not get tired and it does not forget. What it needs is someone to build it around how your business actually works. That is what we do.
