Our story

The phone was never the job.

Why service businesses lose work they already earned, and what we do about it.

01The story02The approach03The builds

01The story

A service business has always been two jobs.

Job one

The work

Fences, roofs, wiring, drains. The thing you are good at, and the reason customers call.

Job two

The desk

Answering, booking, quoting, chasing, invoicing, asking for the review. None of it is the trade. All of it decides whether you get paid for the trade.

Most shops only have someone for the first one.

Then the desk got bigger.

Leads used to come from one phone. Now they come from calls, texts, forms, Facebook, Google and lead sites, at any hour, and each one expects an answer in minutes.

Six ways in. One person to answer them.

  • 78%

    of people have taken their business elsewhere after a company didn’t pick up.

    CallRail, 2025
  • 21x

    more likely to qualify a lead when you reach them within 5 minutes instead of 30.

    MIT / InsideSales lead response study
  • 69%

    of missed business calls never get a callback within 48 hours.

    Quo, 16M+ calls, 2026

And it all lands on someone who is on a roof.

Leads don’t wait for a good time. They come while you are on a job, at dinner, and after you have gone to bed.

An illustration of a working day, not measured data.

Five things the desk needs that a busy owner can’t give it.

  1. SpeedA reply while they still care, not tonight.
  2. CoverageNights, weekends, and every hour you are on a job.
  3. MemoryThe quote from three weeks ago. The invoice from last month.
  4. HandoffsCall to calendar to quote to invoice to review. Every step is a place to drop it.
  5. TimeYours. There is only so much of it.

None of that is skilled work. All of it costs jobs.

02The approach

The desk can run without a person now.

AI can read a message, answer it, book the time, draft the estimate, chase the quote and ask for the review. It does not get tired and it does not forget. What it needs is someone to build it around how your business actually works. That is what we do.

01Find it first

We walk your business from the first call to the review and find where jobs and hours are going. It is free, and you keep the plan.

02Build only that

We build what the audit calls for and nothing else. No packages. Some businesses need two things, some need twelve.

03Then it runs

Everything runs in one app on your phone. We watch it, and you see what needs you today.

No ads

Everything works on leads and customers you already have.

No packages

You get what your audit calls for.

Nothing hidden

Every reply, booking and follow-up is in your app.
