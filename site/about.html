About

One founder. A few clients at a time.

Cinder Revenue Company audits service businesses, then fixes what’s costing them jobs.

Most service businesses already get enough leads. The jobs are lost after the phone rings: calls that go unanswered, estimates that never get booked, quotes nobody chases.

We start every engagement with a free audit. We walk your business from the first call to the review, show you where jobs slip and what each leak is worth, and you keep the plan whether or not you work with us.

If you do, we build only the fixes your business needs, and they all run in one app you can check from your phone.

“I build and watch every client’s system myself, and I only take on a few businesses at a time. If it isn’t a fit, I’ll say so.”Sam Ward, founder, Colorado

How we work.

  1. Audit first.

    We find what’s costing you jobs before we build anything.

  2. Only what you need.

    Every build is custom to your business.

  3. One app.

    Everything we build runs in one place you can check from your phone.

  4. No contract.

    Month to month, cancel by text with 30 days’ notice.

How I work.

01

The person you talk to builds it

No account managers and no handoffs. I scope it, build it and set it up.

02

I watch every system

I check each client’s setup myself, so a problem gets caught before it costs you a job.

03

A few businesses at a time

I cap how many I take on, so each one gets real attention.

04

If it isn’t a fit, I’ll say so

The audit is free either way.

Based in Colorado. Working with service businesses anywhere in the US.
