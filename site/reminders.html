The problem

A no-show costs you the drive and the slot. People book an estimate, then life happens. Nobody reminds them, they forget or go with someone else, and you find out in their driveway.

How you know you have it

  1. Driving to an empty house
  2. Calling the day before to confirm, when you remember
  3. Reschedules that turn into never
What changes

The people on your calendar show up. A reminder costs nothing.

  • You show up to an empty house.They confirmed this morning.
  • Rescheduling takes three calls.They moved it themselves.
  • A cancelled slot stays empty.It gets offered to someone else.
