01 The problem

A no-show costs you the drive and the slot.

People book an estimate, then life happens. Nobody reminds them, they forget or go with someone else, and you find out in their driveway.

How you know you have it

Sign 1

Driving to an empty house

Sign 2

Calling the day before to confirm, when you remember

Sign 3

Reschedules that turn into never

A no-show costs you the drive and the slot. A reminder costs nothing.

02 How it works

From booked to the knock on the door.

Here is what it does, in order. The drawing at the top of the page shows the same thing happening.

  1. FirstConfirmation on booking

    Time, address and what to expect.

  2. ThenReminders before the visit

    The day before and the morning of.

  3. NextOne-tap reschedule

    They pick a new time. Your calendar updates.

  4. LastFreed slots get refilled

    A cancellation is offered to the next lead waiting.
