Most complaints start with not knowing what is going on.

What the customer sees, message by message.

  1. On our wayWith a realistic arrival time.
  2. Running lateThey hear it from you first.
  3. Job startedThe crew taps once, or sends a text.
  4. Job finishedWith photos if you take them.
The problem

Customers who don’t know what is happening start calling. Most complaints are not about the work. They are about waiting at home with no idea when anyone will arrive.

How you know you have it

  1. “Where are you” calls in the middle of a job
  2. Customers taking a day off for a two-hour visit
  3. Finished jobs the customer was the last to hear about
What changes

They know before they have to ask. Fewer “where are you” calls, because they already know.

  • The customer calls to ask where you are.They were told twenty minutes ago.
  • A delay becomes a bad review.A delay becomes a heads-up.
  • The job ends and nobody says so.They get a done message with photos.
