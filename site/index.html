How this is worked out

Calls: missed calls × the share that are new customers (you set this) × 45% who would have booked an estimate if someone had answered. The 45% comes from Invoca's 2026 home-services benchmarks: 45% of home-service phone leads book on the call. Forms and Facebook: leads that wait over an hour × the same 45% × half lost to a faster company. The "half lost" is our working assumption; in Invoca's 2026 consumer survey, 79% said they'd switch to a business that replies faster. Then: estimates × your close rate × your job size. The pays-for-itself line is $997 ÷ (job size × close rate), rounded up. These are estimates, not promises.

What owners ask us.

Do I sign a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel by text with 30 days' notice.

When do I start paying monthly?

Setup is charged when you sign. Monthly starts the day your first booked appointment lands. The Reactivation Sprint is a one-time fee.

What counts as a booked appointment?

An estimate, inspection, consult or service visit with a homeowner in your service area, for a job type you told us you want, with a confirmed date and time on your calendar, from a lead our system contacted. Each household counts once. A homeowner who cancels without rebooking or doesn't show doesn't count. An appointment you cancel or move does count.

Does my number change?

No. Customers call the same number, and calls forward to us only when you don't pick up. On Full Front Office, the AI answers every call and transfers to you when needed.

Can I jump into a conversation?

Yes. You get a text when an appointment books or someone asks for you. Reply and the automation steps back.

What about family and suppliers?

They go on a do-not-text list and never get a text.

Is texting my old customers legal?

We only text people who contacted you, from your business name, with opt-out on every message, and we confirm consent with you before anything goes out.

Which plan is right for me?

Most shops start with Book & Close. If your software already follows up on quotes, Never Miss a Lead covers the leads it misses. Busy shops with a lot of calls choose Full Front Office. Carpet cleaners, detailers and other repeat-service businesses usually start with a Reactivation Sprint.

Does it work for my trade?

Yes. At setup we build it around your trade: the questions you ask, how you price, what gets booked and what comes straight to you. Fence, concrete, roofing, remodeling, HVAC, plumbing, cleaning and more.

Does it speak Spanish?

Yes. It replies in English or Spanish, whichever your customer uses.

How much of my time does it take?

About 30 minutes at the intake call, plus approving your messages.

What happens if I cancel?

We send you every contact, conversation and booked appointment within 3 business days, and transfer the texting number to you or forward it for 60 days. You keep your main business number.

How fast is setup?

Most systems go live within 14 days of your intake call and texting approval. We register your texting number the day you sign, and approval usually takes 1–2 weeks. On Full Front Office, if we're not live in 14 days, your setup fee comes back.

What does the AI actually do?

It answers the calls you miss by voice and replies to every form or message in your approved wording within seconds. It asks your questions, sends your price range, offers open times from your calendar, and books the appointment. Anything sensitive, like pricing or a complaint, goes straight to you. You can read every conversation and take over at any time.

Will my customers know it's automated?

Replies use wording you approve and mention the customer's actual project. If someone asks, it says it's your assistant and offers a real person. Pricing, complaints and anything unusual go straight to you.

Do I have to change my software?

No. We connect to the phone, website forms and calendar you already use. If you don't have a CRM, we set one up as part of the build.

What happens on the 15-minute call?

We ask how leads reach you today and what happens after hours, run your numbers, and tell you whether the guarantee applies to you. No slides and no pressure. If it isn't a fit, we'll tell you.
