What owners ask.

Do I sign a contract?

No. Month to month, cancel by text with 30 days' notice.

When do I start paying monthly?

Setup is paid when you sign. Monthly starts the day your first booked appointment lands. The Reactivation Sprint is a one-time fee.

Does my number change?

No. Customers call the same number you have now. Calls you don't pick up get a text back from your business.

Will customers know it's automated?

Replies use wording you approve and mention the customer's actual project. If someone asks, it says it's your assistant and offers a real person. Pricing questions, complaints and anything unusual come straight to you.

Can I jump into a conversation?

Any time. You get a text when an appointment books or someone asks for you. Reply and the automation steps back.

What about family and suppliers?

They go on a do-not-text list and never get a text.

Do I have to change my software?

No. It works alongside Jobber, Housecall Pro or whatever you use now. If you don't have anything, we set it up.

Does it answer calls by voice?

Not yet. Today every missed call gets an instant text, which most people answer. Voice answering is rolling out, and it's included in your plan when it does.

How fast is setup?

Usually live within 14 days of your intake call. The texting number is registered the day you sign, and carrier approval takes 1–2 weeks.

What if I cancel?

You get every contact, conversation and booked appointment within 3 business days, and you keep your main business number.

What happens on the 15-minute call?

We look at how leads reach you today, run your numbers, and tell you whether the guarantee applies. No slides, no pressure. If it's not a fit, I'll say so.
