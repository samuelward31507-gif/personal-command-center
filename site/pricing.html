What counts toward the 60-second guarantee

Measured from the moment a lead reaches Cinder (a missed call, a text, a website form, or a request from Angi, Thumbtack or Facebook) to when our first reply is sent. Every reply time is logged and shown in your monthly report. If more than 1 in 100 leads in a month gets its first reply later than 60 seconds, that month is free. It doesn’t cover leads that never reach us because call forwarding or a form was disconnected on your side, or delays inside the phone carriers after we send. The free month is a service credit, not a refund.

What counts toward the booked-appointment guarantee

Book & Close: at least 5 booked appointments in your first 30 days live, or at least 3 if you get 10 to 29 new leads a month. What counts: an estimate, inspection or consult with a homeowner in your service area, for a job type you want, with a confirmed time on your calendar, from a lead our system contacted. Each household counts once. A homeowner who cancels without rebooking or doesn’t show doesn’t count; an appointment you cancel or move does. It applies when you connect missed-call forwarding and your lead forms within 3 business days, get 10+ new leads a month, keep open times on your calendar each week, and give us your price sheet. The free month is a service credit, not a refund.
