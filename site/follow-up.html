The problem

A quote nobody chases is a job you gave away. Most people don’t say no. They get busy, mean to reply, and then book whoever checks in. Following up is easy to skip when you are on a job.

What’s included

One text from you. The rest runs.

  • Send a quote with one text and the follow-up starts on its own.
  • “Too expensive” gets your financing or a smaller option. Real price talk comes to you.
  • Invoices and payment reminders sent when the job’s done.
  • Customers pay through your own pay link. We never touch your money.
  • Past customers get a text when they’re due for their next job, with your open times.

Open quotes, old leads, past customers. One text at launch can bring them back. A one-time campaign when you go live, if your audit calls for it.
