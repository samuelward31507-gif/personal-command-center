The problem

A quote nobody chases is a job you gave away. Most people don’t say no. They get busy, mean to reply, and then book whoever checks in. Following up is easy to skip when you are on a job.

How you know you have it

  1. Quotes sent and never mentioned again
  2. Customers who say they forgot
  3. No list of what is still open

Every quote, followed up. Days 2, 5 and 10, then monthly, until it’s a yes or a no.
