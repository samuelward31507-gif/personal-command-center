Billing, cancellation and data

The Booked-Appointment Guarantee

Book & Close: if you don't get at least 5 booked appointments in your first 30 days after go-live, your next monthly payment is waived and we keep working. Full Front Office: the same, with at least 10.

A booked appointment = an estimate, inspection, consult or service visit with a homeowner in your service area, for a job type you told us you want, with a confirmed date and time on your calendar, from a lead our system contacted. Each household counts once. A homeowner who cancels without rebooking or doesn't show doesn't count. An appointment you cancel or move does count.

Applies when you: (1) connect missed-call forwarding and lead forms within 3 business days of signing, (2) have 30+ new leads a month (40+ for Full Front Office), (3) keep calendar availability current with open slots each week, (4) give us your price sheet.

The waived payment is a service credit, not a refund.

60-second guarantee

Reactivation Sprint guarantee

Go-live guarantee
