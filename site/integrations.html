Integrations.

It works with what you already use.

One lead, start to finish. Nothing gets copied over by hand.

  1. A call, a text, your website form, Angi, Thumbtack or a Google adIt comes in from anywhere
  2. Answered in seconds, one thread per customerIt lands in one inbox
  3. Google Calendar, and Jobber if you use itThe estimate goes on your calendar
  4. Invoices and payments land in QuickBooks, on the right customerThe money shows up in your books

Connected both ways. You approve it once. After that, everything lands where it belongs.

Google Calendar
Leads only see times you’re actually free, and every booked estimate lands on your calendar with the address and the job.
Jobber
Every estimate we book shows up in Jobber as a request on that customer’s record. Nobody retypes a booking.
QuickBooks Online
Every invoice you send and every payment you record lands in your books, on the right customer.

Every lead source, one inbox. Wherever a lead comes from, it gets a reply in seconds and shows up in the same place.

Your website form
WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Google Forms and the rest. Form fills come straight in.
Angi, Thumbtack, Google and Yelp
Forward the lead emails once. Each lead is read, saved and answered for you.
Google Ads lead forms
Ad leads go straight to the inbox instead of sitting in a spreadsheet.
Missed calls
A missed call on your business number gets a text back right away, from that same number.
Zapier and Make
Anything else you use can send leads in too.

Get paid your way. We never hold your money.

Your own pay link
Square, Stripe, PayPal or your own site. Invoices and deposits go out with the link you already use.

Moving over is the easy part. Bring your customers, jobs and history with you. Duplicates are merged, and every merge is shown so you can undo it.

Questions.

Do I have to stop using Jobber?

No. Cinder can run beside it or replace it. The audit shows which makes sense for you.

Who connects everything?

We do, during setup. You approve each connection once.

Don’t see a tool you use?

Ask on your audit call. If it can send an email or a form, it can almost always send leads in.
