Integrations

Keep the tools you already use.

Cinder plugs into your calendar, your books and the places your leads come from, so nothing gets copied over by hand.

Connected both ways.

You approve it once. After that, everything lands where it belongs.

  • Google Calendar

    Leads only see times you’re actually free, and every booked estimate lands on your calendar with the address and the job.

  • Jobber

    Every estimate we book shows up in Jobber as a request on that customer’s record. Nobody retypes a booking.

  • QuickBooks Online

    Every invoice you send and every payment you record lands in your books, on the right customer.

Every lead source, one inbox.

Wherever a lead comes from, it gets a reply in seconds and shows up in the same place.

  • Your website form

    WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Google Forms and the rest. Form fills come straight in.

  • Angi, Thumbtack, Google and Yelp

    Forward the lead emails once. Each lead is read, saved and answered for you.

  • Google Ads lead forms

    Ad leads go straight to the inbox instead of sitting in a spreadsheet.

  • Missed calls

    A missed call on your business number gets a text back right away, from that same number.

  • Zapier and Make

    Anything else you use can send leads in too.

Get paid your way.

We never hold your money.

  • Your own pay link

    Square, Stripe, PayPal or your own site. Invoices and deposits go out with the link you already use.

Moving over is the easy part.

Bring your customers, jobs and history with you. Duplicates are merged, and every merge is shown so you can undo it.

  • Jobber and QuickBooks

    Pulled in directly, photos included.

  • Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan

    From their standard exports.

  • Any spreadsheet

    Customers, payments and service plans.

  • Your phone’s contacts

    Customers without an email still come in.
