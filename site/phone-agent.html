The problem

A missed call is usually a missed job.

A caller who reaches voicemail often hangs up and tries the next name on the list. You never see the job you lost.

How you’d know

  • Missed calls on your phone when you come down the ladder
  • Voicemails you return hours later
  • Callers who had already booked someone else

Two minutes on the phone. Ten seconds to read.
