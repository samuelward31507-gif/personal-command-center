App questions.

Anything else, ask it on the call.

Does the app cost extra?

No. It comes with Never Miss a Lead and Book & Close. Quote follow-ups and invoices sent for you are part of Book & Close.

Do I have to stop using Jobber or Housecall Pro?

No. Cinder works alongside them. If you’d rather run everything here, we move your customers, quotes and jobs in for you, and you can undo the move for 7 days.

Is there a phone app?

It runs on any phone. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with notifications when a lead needs you.

Can my crew use it?

Yes. Each crew member gets their own login with their jobs, directions, checklists and photos. They never see prices or money.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

It’s yours. Export every customer, quote, job, invoice and payment any time, and we hand you everything when you leave.
