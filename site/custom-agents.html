Custom / Custom agents

An agent built for one job in your business.

Some work does not fit a standard build. We make an AI agent that does that one job the way you do it, with your rules and your tools.

The problem1 of 5

There is a job in your business that eats hours and never needed you.

Checking supplier quotes, chasing permits, updating the job board. It follows the same steps every time, and it still lands on your desk.

How you know you have it

Sign 1

A task you could explain to a new hire in ten minutes

Sign 2

The same steps, every day

Sign 3

Work that waits because you are the one who does it

If you can explain the job to a new hire, we can probably build it.

How it works2 of 5

One task, done your way, inside limits you set.

Here is what it does, in order. The drawing at the top of the page shows the same thing happening.

Step 1Built around one task

Ordering materials, checking permits, answering supplier emails, whatever keeps landing on you.

Step 2It uses your tools

Your inbox, your calendar, your job software.

Step 3It asks before it acts

On anything you mark as needing a person.

Step 4You can see what it did

Every step is logged in your app.

What changes3 of 5

It runs. You check what it did.

Nothing about how you run jobs has to change. This is the difference on an ordinary day.

Without it

  • The task waits for you.
  • You hire for it.
  • Nobody knows how it is done but you.

With it

  • The task is done when you look.
  • You build it once.
  • It is written down and running.

Getting set up4 of 5

A short list from you. The rest is on us.

We build it around the tools and habits you already have, and you see it working before it touches a real customer.

What we need from you

  • An hour to walk us through the job
  • Access to the tools it touches
  • The limits it has to stay inside

What we take care of

  • Scope it in writing
  • Build it and test it with you
  • Watch it run and adjust

It starts with the free audit. If the audit does not call for this build, we will say so.

Questions5 of 5

What owners ask first.

The short answers.

What can an agent do?

Read, write, look things up, fill in forms and use the tools you connect. It works inside limits you set.

What will it not do?

Spend money or send anything you have marked for approval without asking.

Is this part of a package?

No. There are no packages. We build what your audit calls for and nothing else.
