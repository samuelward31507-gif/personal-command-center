The problem

A lead who says yes still has to get on your calendar. That takes a call back, a few texts about times, and a reminder nobody sends. Every extra step is a chance for them to go quiet.

How you know you have it

  1. Phone tag to find a time
  2. Estimates nobody confirmed
  3. Empty driveways

One lead, from the form to the driveway.
