01 The problem

Happy customers would send a neighbor. Almost none are asked.

The moment to ask is right after they tell you they are pleased. That moment passes while you are loading the truck.

How you know you have it

Sign 1

Sign 2

Sign 3

02 How it works

One customer, asked once, becomes a street.

Here is what it does, in order. The drawing at the top of the page shows the same thing happening.

Step 1Asked at the right moment

Right after they have told you they are happy.

Step 2Easy to pass on

A link or a card they can forward.

Step 3Tracked

You know who sent whom.

Step 4A thank-you that goes out

Whatever you choose to offer, sent without you remembering.

03 Getting set up

A short list from you. The rest is on us.

We build it around the tools and habits you already have, and you see it working before it touches a real customer.

What we need from you

  • Your customer list, however messy
  • What you want to be known for
  • A yes before anything is sent

What we take care of

  • Clean the list up
  • Write the messages in your voice
  • Honor every opt-out
