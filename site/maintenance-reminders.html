The problem

Repeat work is due on a date nobody is tracking. Staining, servicing, seasonal checks. The customer does not remember when it is due and neither do you, so somebody else gets asked.

How you know you have it

  1. Maintenance work that only comes when the customer remembers
  2. No record of what is due when
  3. A quiet off-season

A steady off-season. Repeat work fills the weeks new jobs do not.
